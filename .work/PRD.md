# PRD: Mr. Ahmed Samir - 3D Luxury English Teacher Portfolio

## 1. Executive Summary & Vision
An ultra-luxurious, bespoke 3D portfolio and educational showcase for **Mr. Ahmed Samir**, premier English Language Educator for Preparatory and Secondary students (إعدادي وثانوي). Designed to break all generic AI template cliches, utilizing high-end editorial aesthetics (Dark Espresso Obsidian, Warm Cognac, Champagne Amber Gold, and Alabaster White), kinetic 3D typography, 2.5D magnetic portrait parallax, and interactive phonetics audio tools.

## 2. Target Audience & Core Value Proposition
- **High School & Prep Students (إعدادي وثانوي)**: Seeking top scores, exam tricks mastery, engaging modern education, and exam confidence.
- **Parents**: Seeking verified prestige, master expertise (12+ years), track record of top rankers (أوائل المحافظة والجمهورية), and disciplined follow-up.

## 3. Visual & Aesthetic Architecture
- **Palette**: Dark Espresso Obsidian (`#070605`, `#0D0A08`), Warm Mocha (`#1A1512`), Cognac Bronze (`#8C4D2B`), Champagne Amber Glow (`#D4A373`), Alabaster White (`#FAF6F0`).
- **Typography**: Playfair Display (Serif Editorial Display) + Plus Jakarta Sans & Alexandria (Arabic/English UI).
- **3D & Motion**: Subtle floating 3D kinetic alphabet and phonetic characters, magnetic cursor tilt, and luminous rim-lighting.

## 4. Key Modules & Functional Scope
1. **Hero Experience**: 2.5D magnetic tilt portrait with dynamic golden rim-light, floating glass badges, and 3D WebGL background letters.
2. **Methodology & Pillars (Bento Grid)**: 4 pillars of mastery (Grammar Mastery, Phonetics & Listening, Exam Mastery & Tricks, Individual Follow-up).
3. **Curricula & Academic Levels**: 1st, 2nd, 3rd Prep & 1st, 2nd, 3rd Secondary (Thanawya Amma).
4. **Interactive Phonetics & Accent Lab**: Live audio pronunciation & interactive comparison (British vs. American / Tricky words).
5. **Hall of Fame (لوحة الشرف)**: Top ranking students, test scores, and student testimonials with celebration effects.
6. **Booking & Centers Hub**: Direct booking via WhatsApp, center locations, schedules, and online platform access.
